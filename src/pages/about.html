Why Hash Technologies Exists

Hash Technologies develops reliable, developer-friendly software solutions that combine AI-assisted candidate parsing with clean, transparent applicant pipelines. We build systems that organizations can deploy in days, not quarters.

Platform Architecture Overview

Ingestion & Document Parsing Subsystem ▼

Converts PDFs, Word documents, and text files into standard JSON structures, extracting contact records, employment histories, and key qualifications.

Multi-Model AI Scoring Layer ▼

Assesses candidate competency matrices against customizable role rubrics using modular LLM adapters, producing transparent scoring rationale.

Secure Webhook & Notification Engine ▼

Dispatches instant payloads to internal communication channels, applicant email notification queues, and external ERPs when pipeline state updates occur.
